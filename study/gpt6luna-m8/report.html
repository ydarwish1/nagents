Do more agents actually help?

model subagents:gpt-6-luna-low,no-tools,10-per-batch · topology independent · 40 trials per size · seed 21 · nagents 0.3.0

One agent was right 75.0% of the time; a group of 9 voting was right 97.5%. The data rules out any real gain beyond 7 agents. But seats were not equally good (alone, they ranged from 57.5% to 75.0%), so the fairer curve counts every seat equally: 64.4% for one agent, 97.5% for 9.

Shared mistakes

Across 1440 pairs of agents answering the same problem alone, the error correlation was +0.05 (0 means mistakes are independent, 1 means agents are always wrong together). When two agents were both wrong, they gave the same wrong number 4.1% of the time. Voting only helps when mistakes don't line up.
