Do more agents actually help?

model subagents:haiku,no-tools,10-per-batch · topology independent · 40 trials per size · seed 21 · nagents 0.3.0

One agent was right 72.5% of the time; a group of 9 voting was right 92.5%. Accuracy was still rising at the largest group measured. But seats were not equally good (alone, they ranged from 17.5% to 72.5%), so the fairer curve counts every seat equally: 37.8% for one agent, 91.1% for 9.

Shared mistakes

Across 1440 pairs of agents answering the same problem alone, the error correlation was +0.02 (0 means mistakes are independent, 1 means agents are always wrong together). When two agents were both wrong, they gave the same wrong number 0.4% of the time. Voting only helps when mistakes don't line up.
