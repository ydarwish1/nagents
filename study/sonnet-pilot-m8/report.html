Shared mistakes

Across 100 pairs of agents answering the same problem alone, the error correlation was -0.02 (0 means mistakes are independent, 1 means agents are always wrong together). When two agents were both wrong, they gave the same wrong number – of the time. Voting only helps when mistakes don't line up.
